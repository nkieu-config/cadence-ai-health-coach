Which AI, and why · the approach

06 / 09

Code handles what must be exact · the LLM what must sound human

Our code — no AI

  • Counts 10 pairings of sleep, food, movement, disruptors
  • Speaks only when groups differ ≥ 20% with ≥ 3 days each
  • Under 7 days, nothing is sent to the AI at all

The LLM — Gemini

  • Turns those numbers into language that does not judge
  • Answers in the context of that specific user
  • Proposes a small step tied to a real calendar

Anything the system writes itself — analysis, goals, reflection — passes one more filter: no weight, no body shape, no calories, no fasting · failures fall back to a standard sentence

The AI never touches a number — if it goes down the numbers remain and no page breaks

07 / 09

We measured three models — and did not pick the newest

Built on a zero baht budget — the free daily quota is a real constraint

In production

Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite

Chat reply 2.2 s

Free quota 500/day

Fast enough, and enough quota for the whole app

Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite

Chat reply about the same

Free quota 500/day

Replies ~40% shorter — not worth changing the coach's voice now

Gemini 3.6 Flash

Chat reply 10.4 s

Free quota 20/day

4× slower · four teammates exhaust it before one analysis

Measured on 22 July with real data and the real pipeline · quotas come from hitting the ceiling, not docs · switching models is one environment variable

Limits · privacy · what comes next

09 / 09

The limits we know, and how we keep them from misleading users

Every number is self-reported

Guarded by no scores, no streaks — honesty costs nothing

Our only 4 testers built it

Guarded by calling 1:26 a design ceiling, not an average

An LLM is never fully controllable

Guarded by a validator on every goal — failures fall back to a standard goal

Health data — visible only to its owner · deletable any time · no name or email reaches the model
